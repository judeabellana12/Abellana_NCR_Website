Travel Tips

  • Check traffic conditions before leaving for your destination.
  • Use trusted transportation services and follow road safety rules.
  • Bring water, sunscreen, and an umbrella.
  • Keep your belongings secure in crowded areas.
  • Respect local customs and historical landmarks.
  • Check the opening hours and entrance fees of attractions in advance.
  • Prepare extra money for transportation and unexpected expenses.
