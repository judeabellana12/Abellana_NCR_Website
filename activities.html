There is always something interesting to do in Metro Manila.

🏛️

History & Culture

Discover the stories and traditions behind the capital.

  • Explore the historic walls of Intramuros.
  • Visit Fort Santiago and its historical exhibits.
  • Learn about Philippine art at the National Museum.
  • Visit historic churches and heritage buildings.
🌳

Outdoor Activities

Enjoy open spaces and relaxing outdoor experiences.

  • Walk around Rizal Park.
  • Take photographs of landmarks and city views.
  • Explore public parks and gardens.
  • Enjoy a relaxing afternoon with family or friends.
🍜

Food & Dining

Discover the flavors of Filipino food and local dining.

  • Try Filipino favorites such as adobo and sinigang.
  • Enjoy traditional Filipino desserts.
  • Explore local restaurants and food markets.
  • Sample popular snacks and street food from reputable vendors.
🛍️

Shopping & City Life

Experience the modern lifestyle of the capital region.

  • Explore shopping centers and local stores.
  • Discover public art around Bonifacio Global City.
  • Visit cafes and restaurants.
  • Enjoy the modern city atmosphere of Metro Manila.

Make Every Visit Memorable

Plan your activities based on your interests, available time, and budget. Respect local communities and keep tourist destinations clean.
